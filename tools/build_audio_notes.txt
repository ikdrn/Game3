Procedural Gunshot Notes
=======================

AudioManager.gd synthesizes gunshots with:
1) White noise burst
2) Exponential decay envelope
3) Harmonic sine-frequency mix

Profiles:
- AR: duration 0.12, decay 40
- Pistol: duration 0.15, decay 35
- Shotgun: duration 0.25, decay 20
- Sniper: duration 0.35, decay 15

No binary audio files are used.
